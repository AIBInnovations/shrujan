Stand where it is made — the studio, the museum and the villages.

The Shrujan Campus

A behind-the-scenes visit, arranged in advance. You follow a piece from an idea through sampling and into production, and see how the design studio and the artisan communities actually work together. It is a visit about process, not a shop.

  • Design studio & sampling
  • Pre and post production
  • The coordination team

02

Any time in season

Kutch itself

The district the work comes from: craft villages within an hour of Bhuj, a museum built for the embroidery, and a landscape that turns to salt flat and back again. Come for a day, or plan a week around it.

  • Craft villages & workshops
  • The LLDC museum
  • Palaces, port towns, the Rann
Plan your trip

Where to go

Six stops, all near Bhuj

The workrooms, the museum, and the villages the stitches are named after — none of them more than a couple of hours apart.

01 Bhuj · by appointment The Shrujan Campus The design studio, sampling rooms and production floor, 10am to 7pm.
02 Ajrakhpur · 20 min from Bhuj LLDC Museum The Living & Learning Design Centre — galleries, archive and teaching studios. Read more
03 Bhujodi · 8 km east Bhujodi Weavers Pit looms in working homes, weaving wool and cotton for the desert winter.
04 Ajrakhpur Ajrakhpur Block Printers Resist printing in madder and indigo, sixteen stages deep, done in the open air.
05 Northern Kutch The Banni Villages Suf, Mutava and Jat embroidery, in the grasslands the stitches are named after.
06 Mandvi and the western edge Vijay Vilas & Lakhpat A seaside palace and a walled port town the sea left behind.

How long to stay

Plan the trip

  1. 01 Half a day The campus in the morning, LLDC in the afternoon.
  2. 02 One day Add the Bhujodi looms and an hour in the Bhuj bazaar.
  3. 03 Two days Ajrakhpur printing and a Banni village, with an interpreter.
  4. 04 A week All of it, plus Mandvi, Lakhpat and the Rann at full moon.
