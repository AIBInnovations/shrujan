The future of heritage is here

Craft that cares: four promises in every piece

Living embroidery, natural cloth and honest making, held to the same four standards since 1969.

  1. 01

    Hand Embroidery

    Every motif counted and stitched by hand. No machine ever touches the cloth.

  2. 02

    Natural Dyes

    Madder, indigo and pomegranate on handloom cotton and mulberry silk.

  3. 03

    Fair Wages

    Paid per piece, on time, directly to the woman whose hands made it.

  4. 04

    Made to Last

    Finished to be worn today, repaired for free, and handed down tomorrow.

Our Craft

Every stitch carries
a signature

Shrujan began in 1969 with Chanda Shroff, known as Kaki, who went to Kutch after a famine and found women whose embroidery was extraordinary and whose earnings were not. She turned that skill into a livelihood.

From her vision to the hands of thousands of women across Kutch, Shrujan has quietly safeguarded the region’s living craft traditions for over five decades, preserving the knowledge and the dignity behind it.

Help & Support

Everything you need to know

Thoughtful answers for a seamless shopping experience.

Yes. Every garment is embroidered by artisan women across forty villages of Kutch. No machine embroidery, ever. Each piece carries the name of the hands that made it.

Our pieces come in XS to XXL, and every silhouette is cut to drape generously. Made-to-measure is available on request for saris, lehengas and bridal commissions.

Ready pieces ship from Bhuj within 48 hours and arrive in 3–6 days across India. Made-to-order embroidery takes 2–3 weeks. Good things, stitched slowly.

We ship worldwide. International orders arrive in 8–14 days, fully tracked, with duties settled at checkout so there are no surprises.

Easy returns and exchanges within 14 days for unworn pieces with tags intact. Hand-embroidered commissions and altered garments are made to order and non-returnable.
