Studio Collection

Three strands, three ways in

  • 02 Avinya Artisans in teams of two and three, co-creating original work. Open

01

Aabhla · Kutch

The Kanchli, reworked

The Kutchi bodice cut for a modern silhouette, the mirror-work kept exactly as it has always been stitched. Sixty hours on the frame before a single seam is closed.

  • Hand-embroidered
  • 60 hours
  • Edition of 24

02

Bandhani · Kutch

Bandhani, tied by hand

Thousands of points tied one at a time before the cloth ever meets dye. The pattern only reveals itself when the knots come loose — no two lengths finish alike.

  • Natural dye
  • 3 weeks
  • Edition of 18
Enquire about this piece
03

Suf · Kutch

Suf, counted from the reverse

Suf is worked from the back of the cloth, counted thread by thread, with no pattern ever drawn. The embroiderer holds the whole geometry in her head.

  • Counted thread
  • No pattern drawn
  • Edition of 12
Enquire about this piece
04

Handloom · Bhujodi

Handloom, woven in Bhujodi

Woven on pit looms a few kilometres from the atelier, in wool and cotton spun for the desert winter. It softens with every wash rather than fading.

  • Pit loom
  • Wool & cotton
  • Edition of 30
