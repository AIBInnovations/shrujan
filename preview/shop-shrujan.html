Why it costs what it costs

01

She is paid per piece, on time

Directly, and at a rate she agreed to. That arrangement was rare in 1969 and it still is.

02

Nothing is rushed

A kanchli takes sixty hours. A bridal odhani takes months. We quote the real time, not a delivery promise.

03

It is repaired, not replaced

Send it back whenever it needs work. Alterations and repairs are free for as long as you own it.
